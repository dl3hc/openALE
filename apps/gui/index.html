Propagation-aware LQA scoring weights recent channel measurements by solar-elevation similarity. When enabled, channels measured under similar ionospheric conditions (same time of day) receive a scoring bonus of up to 2×. SFI (10.7 cm solar flux) refines the scoring via NOAA SWPC daily data. Listen-before-transmit window. 784 ms for ALE-only channels, ≥ 2 s for shared channels (set per channel).
Delay after PTT key-up before audio transmits — covers CAT/CI-V latency. Default 10 ms covers typical SDR/CAT hardware; without it the first word (your own full callsign) can be clipped before the radio has actually keyed, confusing the other station. The protocol's handshake timing windows are only tens of ms wide, so values much above ~10 ms can silently break handshaking with other stations entirely — only raise it for demonstrably slower CAT/PTT hardware.
Delay after RX re-enable before PTT releases — allows audio buffer to drain. Default 200 ms covers typical SDR/CAT output latency so the last word isn't clipped. Lower it only if your radio switches to RX faster than that.

LBT Busy Detection
Listen-before-transmit occupancy check (A.5.4.7.2): blocks TX when broadband energy (voice, data, carrier — not just ALE) exceeds the tracked noise floor by the margin. Raise the margin at noisy locations so local QRM never blocks transmissions; the noise floor itself adapts to steady local noise automatically.
dB
LBT Override
Operator override (A.5.4.7.3, emergency use): ignore busy-channel results — transmit even when the channel appears occupied. The LBT pause itself still runs.
Scan Stop Squelch
Optional sensitivity control for the scan-stop detector (§A.5.3.3). Off (default): stop on any ALE signal. On: only stop when a signal sits at least the margin above the auto-calibrated audio-path noise floor — so scanning ignores weak traffic. Calibrate snapshots the current floor. Higher margin ≈ stronger signal required (3 dB ≈ any decodable signal, 6 dB ≈ fair, 10 dB ≈ solid).
